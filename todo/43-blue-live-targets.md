# 43 — Blue: fill the slots with live, paying targets

Updated 2026-10-24 (Marc + Claude). Read `CLAUDE.md` first, then [blue-reporting](../docs/06-strategy/blue-reporting.md) (esp. §8, §9) and [rival-census-2026-10-23](../docs/06-strategy/rival-census-2026-10-23.md). Code: `src/swan_blue/{service,evidence,detect,side}.py`, `swan-blue.service` on the box. Blue is a separate budget (report routes 3/min each) and uses no writes and no voices, and it stays ×1: it is the cheapest place to add points on the ×1 days while Red coasts, and it must never take Red's resources on the ×4 days.

Yesterday (23 Oct) Blue made about 1.5k. Realistic with this work: **2.5–3k a day**. The ceiling is the slots (30 pending × 3 h ≈ 240 reports a day), the per-team cap (below) and the supply of live targets. Not 5×.

## The rules this rests on (scoring §3, Marc's read 24 Oct)
- Moderator rules 3 h after the first report; **each additional team that reports halves the wait**. Several accounts of one team count as one report (we file from one reporter anyway).
- About 1 ruling in 10 is wrong either way. A **genuine** ruling locks all our reporting for 6 h (pending reports still resolve).
- **Post report:** 25 if our team is first to report it, 5 if later (first five teams only). Competitor posts can be real; a "genuine" ruling pays nothing.
- **Account report:** suspended if, **at the ruling**, it has ≥1 moderator-hidden post **and** ≥100 actions; 15 points to **every** team with an open report at that moment (no first/later difference). If the conditions are not met: dismissed, no team is paid, the account may be reported again, and a report is **never paid retroactively**. A report filed on an account that is already suspended is wasted.
- 3 hidden posts suspend an account. Verified accounts (ids 1–11, organisers) are the exception: the moderator is always right about them; not obtainable, ignore.
- **Per target team per Sydney day:** awards 1–5 full, 6–10 half, 11+ at 10%, posts and accounts together (an upheld account award counts toward it).
- Reports must be truthful and evidence-based, and the detail must never state a post's side (rules §5.2.2, LS.6.5); never our own accounts.

## What the data showed (census, 23 Oct)
- Of our 89 account reports, **46 (52%) were on accounts already idle for more than 3 h when filed** (27 idle over 12 h). All 37 reported while active (last post ≤ 1 h before filing) stopped posting by the ruling. So an account that is live and has a hidden post is upheld almost every time; the 50% in `evidence.ACCOUNT_UPHELD` (=0.5) is the idle waste, not the live rate.
- Supply of live leads is thin: of 189 unreported account leads, **21** had posted in the previous 2 h (15 ready); 168 were idle.
- Blue's `labels` are only 16 clusters (c8701 alone has 450 accounts, over a team's 200 cap), so its per-cluster daily count says "saturated" while the real teams behind it may still be at full rate.

## Work, in order
1. **Liveness filter on account reports.** Before filing, read the account's newest post (`account_posts`, limit 1: the 300/min read route, not the report budget) and skip unless it posted within ~2 h; re-check at filing time (leads go stale between harvest and filing). Log `idle_h` on every account report. Then set `ACCOUNT_UPHELD` for a live account to ~0.9 (idle ones are skipped now), so live accounts (EV about 13) rank above half-rate posts. After a day compare the upheld rate by idle bucket (≤1 h, 1–3 h, 3–12 h, >12 h) from the portal's Moderation lines and set the threshold from that.
2. **Widen the supply of live leads (otherwise 1 only shrinks Blue).** A hidden post vanishes from `account_posts`. Diff two snapshots of the newest posts of live rival accounts (posted in the last ~6 h, 30+ posts, not ours): a post present in the first and missing in the second is a moderator-hidden candidate. An author's own delete looks the same; such an account report is dismissed and costs a slot, never a lock. About 1 read per live account (600–1,500 reads, 10–20 min at 90 a minute); run every 3–4 h inside Blue's read budget (it already uses under 150 other reads a minute of 300 per route). Output: live account leads with a hidden post. The rival census (todo/44) second snapshot is the first test; reuse `scripts/rival_pull.py`.
3. **Slot value ordering.** Per target team the first 5 awards pay in full, so spend those on 25-point first reports of fresh ring posts (when P(genuine) pays for the lock: `service.LOCK_COST`), then fill with live account reports (15, no lock risk). Prefer an account **other teams are already reporting** (a post of theirs vanished a few minutes ago): the ruling comes in 1.5 h or less, the slot turns over twice as fast, and the 15 is paid to every reporter. Past 10 awards on a team, move to another team.
4. **Spread by fleet, not by cluster.** Accounts with neighbouring ids (and creation times) share a fate 74% of the time (51% by chance), so a fleet of ids is a good proxy for a team. Count awards per fleet from the portal log and fill 5 full awards per fleet per day: 40 fleets × 5 is 200 full awards against about 240 slots. Replace or refine `service.counts_today`'s cluster key and the `SPREAD` rank with it. Fleets are built from `data/rival_census/census.jsonl` (ids and creation times) plus `labels`.
5. **Sibling checks.** When one account of a live fleet gets a hidden post, check its siblings for vanished posts first (extends `widen`, which today follows rings only).

## Limits (read before building more)
- **The hidden-post gate.** A stealthy, productive survivor has no hidden post, so identifying it does not remove it. The only route is a truthful post report on one of its posts, which needs real ring evidence (about 8% genuine rate, each genuine = a 6 h lock). If there is no ring, there is no honest report to file.
- **We cannot aim at the leaderboard.** We cannot map accounts to teams, and volume cannot identify top teams (every team is capped at 3 writes a minute). Do not describe any report as targeting "top teams".
- **Side.** `BLUE_SIDE_BIAS` (yes 1.3, unclear 1.0, no 0.7) is a ranking weight only. Rival No accounts last fewer hours at the same pace but get the same writes per account, so there is no evidence that Yes targets are better or worse; keep the weight, do not rely on it, and never put side in a detail.
- **Spirit.** Prioritising valid, truthful reports by how active and long-lived an account is looks like ordinary prioritisation. Draft for `#questions` if Marc wants it sent: "We prioritise which inauthentic accounts to report by how active they are, and every report states only measured facts. Is that within the spirit of the Blue track?"
