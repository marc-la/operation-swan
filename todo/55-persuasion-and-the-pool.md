## 2. Why the answers matter
- **The 400,000 pool.** It's split over the No side's persuasion on 31 Oct-2 Nov. Our toward weight is about 3-4 units a day:
  - primary toward = 1 each;
  - each contribution = 0.5 ÷ the posts that share it.
- **If "neither side" changes count** (scoring §2: *"Every positively scored vaccination change/contribution also counts
  toward your referendum persuasion share if your side wins"*, and a neither change scores +20):
  - our weight would be **several times larger** (9-20 primary a day plus 143-238 contributions; the exact factor
    depends on how many posts share each contribution);
  - the flip, which seems to produce restatements, would be worth far more than its engagement.
  - The worked example on the page counts only "changes of mind and contributions **towards Vote No**", so this is
    genuinely unclear.
- **If the count weighs certainty and turnout** (scoring §4: *"how certain they are and how likely they are to vote"*):
  - firming No-leaners ([todo/54](54-firm-the-no.md)) also moves the result;
  - and at Poll 3's mix No wins if leaning voters turn out at under 78% of definite ones (situation §5).
- **If early votes are fixed when cast** (in-world early voting opened 28 Oct), persuasion of early voters after they vote
  does nothing for the count.
