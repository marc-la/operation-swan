# 48 — Citizens above id 2343: a classifier, then an arm and Blue's final check

Written 2026-10-27 (Marc + Claude). Read `CLAUDE.md` first, then [targeting-audit-2026-10-27](../docs/06-strategy/targeting-audit-2026-10-27.md) (finding 9). **Nothing here posts, follows, likes or reports.** Build and validate offline; every new switch ships **off**.

## Why
The briefing says "just over 6,000" NPC citizens; `config.is_citizen` knows 2,332 (ids 12-2343, seeded before launch). The organisers said more may be added mid-game, and the evidence says they were:
- Ids **4400-4790** were created 19 Oct 17:00-20:53 UTC at one every ~36 s with real-name display names, the seeded pace (25-42 s, [platform-mechanics](../docs/06-strategy/platform-mechanics.md#citizen-ids-and-the-feed-budget-2026-10-21-todo21)). Sampled accounts there (4516, 4580, 4589, 4592, 4760) post in short sessions every 1-2 days in the citizen style. Near **7701** (20 Oct ~21:00) the same, mixed with deleted/private ids.
- The same bands hold team bots too: **4464** posts almost every hour; 4348-4377 posted polished testimonials ("I'm a nurse...", "I run New Haven's pantry"). So an **id range is not safe**; judge each account.
- Why it matters: the bot drops every post by an id > 2343 ("team bots, ~98% of Latest", `Targeter._consider`), so we may answer only ~40% of the electorate, and our metrics (outcomes, follow-ups, `citizen_replies`) never count those citizens. In a 15-min Latest snapshot, 103 posts were by ids <= 2343 and 829 by higher ids.
- Blue: `swan_blue/evidence.py:33` and `fleets.py:21` call any id > 2343 a competitor. A "genuine" ruling locks all reporting for 6 h; four came on 26 Oct alone. Marc remembers a block of genuine rulings that must have been citizens. **The classifier is a final check there too.**

## Build
1. **Data (read-only).** `account_posts`, `get_account`, `replies` (300/min each, **team-wide, shared with the live bot and swan_blue**: stay under ~60/min, read `rate_limit`, back off on 429). Cache to a file, never re-read an account within a run. The box (`ssh swan-ec2`, read-only) has `logs/`, `data/latest_snapshot.json`, `data/blue_state.json`, `logs/blue_reports.jsonl`. Copies of the action/outcome/follow-up logs: `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-marc-ctn/60015ff6-f5a0-4737-a51c-8b9c68734f6b/scratchpad/` (`actions.jsonl` etc.).
2. **Labels.** Citizens: a random ~300 of ids 12-2343 that posted. Team bots: our own accounts (ids only from `secrets/swan_accounts.json`, never print other fields), authors Blue's moderator ruled **inauthentic** (hidden), and accounts with team names (`civic_voice_08_bc923e`). The authors of posts ruled **genuine** are a separate check set (expected to look citizen-like).
3. **Features**, simple and explainable: posting rhythm (sessions = posts within ~15 min; sessions a day; gap between sessions; posts per session), share of posts in Latest hours, creation pace of the account's id neighbours (a run at 25-42 s, evenly), display name vs username, style (lowercase share, giphy links, length), reply vs post mix, whether they ever reply to a citizen <= 2343 and get answered. Seeded citizens were active from creation and are online 1-5 min every ~2 days.
4. **Model.** A score in [0, 1] and two cuts: `NPC_TARGET_MIN` (for writing to them: favour **precision**, a team bot read as a citizen wastes a write) and `NPC_BLUE_MIN` (for Blue: favour **recall**, any plausible citizen is not reported). Report precision/recall on held-out labels for both cuts, and the score of the genuine-ruled authors.
5. **Census.** Score every account id > 2343 that posted in the last 72 h (from the snapshot plus a Latest-sized sample; no feed reads beyond what the bot already caches: `data/latest_snapshot.json`), and estimate how many citizens exist above 2343 and what share of Latest they write.

## Wire (all off by default)
- `src/swan_bot/npc.py` (pure scoring), `scripts/npc_scan.py` (`--dry` prints, otherwise writes `data/npc_scores.json`; read-only API), tests in `tests/test_npc.py` that need no network.
- `config.likely_citizen(id)`: `is_citizen(id)` **or** the cached score >= `NPC_TARGET_MIN`. `is_citizen` itself stays as it is (every metric uses it).
- Targeting: `NPC_EXTRA_ARM = False`. When on, `_consider` admits likely citizens as candidates in a switchback arm (`("arm_npc", ["base", "extra"], 3 * 3600)` in `SWITCHBACK`, plus `FREEZE_PIN["arm_npc"]`). Logged with a field (`npc_score`) so the read can split them. Outcomes for those writes must count their replies (the metric is points per write from the portal and our proxy, by arm).
- Blue: `BLUE_NPC_GUARD = False`. When on, `evidence.py`/`fleets.py` never treat an account scoring >= `NPC_BLUE_MIN` as a competitor; logged as a skip with its score.

## Read-out
Precision/recall per cut; the citizen count above 2343 and the share of Latest; the genuine-ruled authors' scores against the hidden ones; a dry run of `_consider` on the snapshot (how many more candidates an hour, of which class). **Do not push.** Commit on the worktree branch and report; Marc and the main session turn the flags on.

## Built and read out (27 Oct, subagent; read-only, 1,266 platform reads: 845 `account_posts`, 18 `get_account`, 403 `get_post`)
**Data.** Labels: 320 random seeded citizens (39 of them the snapshots' own authors), 282 with the 2+ sessions a score needs. Team: 601 moderator-hidden authors (Blue's hidden post reports, harvest leads and account reports, minus the genuine-ruled), 55 of ours, 7 `civic_voice_NN_hex` names; 663 scored. Pages: one fresh `account_posts` page each for the citizens, ours and the 441 snapshot authors above 2343 without a recent census page; for the rest, the newest page in `data/rival_census` (`rest.jsonl` 23 Oct and the box's 6-hourly `snap-*.jsonl` to 27 Oct 02:40). Two Latest snapshots (03:30-03:45, 04:00-04:15 UTC, 1,934 posts).

**Model** (`src/swan_bot/npc.py`, `MODEL` dated 2026-10-27): logistic regression on 15 features of one page. Biggest terms: hours between sessions (+), clock hours with a post a day (-), a two-word display name (+), bursts of posts under 2 min apart (+), sessions a day (-). Seeded citizens: 0.8 sessions a day, 3.6 posts a session, 70% of posts under 2 min after the last; team accounts: 9-25 sessions and 12-20 clock hours a day. Left out (weight ~0): the id neighbours' creation pace (the whole platform registered about one account every 30 s on 19-21 Oct), emoji, hex usernames. No score (None) under 3 posts or with one session only: a bot that never pauses for 15 min is not scored either (it stays a team account).

| cut | 5-fold out-of-fold: recall / precision / team accounts above it | 30% held out (97 citizens, 189 team) |
|---|---|---|
| `NPC_BLUE_MIN` 0.3 | 1.000 / 0.993 / 2 of 663 (0.3%) | 1.000 / 1.000 |
| `NPC_TARGET_MIN` 0.9 | 0.996 / 1.000 / 0 of 663 | 1.000 / 1.000 |

The two team accounts at 0.3-0.9: 4041 (0.76, sessions of polished LLM posts a day apart, hidden by the moderator) and 4197 (0.54). Precision on the live population is lower than on these labels: the team labels are the moderator's obvious ring bots and ours; a team that posts in short daily sessions would score like 4041. Eyeballed 6 random accounts above 2343 (4 at >= 0.9, 2 at 0.3-0.9): all read as citizens (first post minutes after creation, off-topic life posts, lowercase, giphy, `@handle` openers).

**Blue.** Genuine-ruled authors (20 scorable of 34): 18 score 0.00-0.05 (team bots; 27 of the 34 had other posts hidden: moderator errors, which come in runs, e.g. 24 Oct 05:48), two score 0.99 and 1.00: 6066 `climbin_with_nicole` and 8993 `mint_moose_daab`, citizens created 20-21 Oct, reported as part of reply rings on 22-23 Oct. Hidden-ruled authors: 2 of 601 at >= 0.3 (in-sample). Replayed on all 599 of our reports, the guard at 0.3 skips exactly those 2 (both genuine) and no hidden or upheld one. The block of 24 Oct genuine rulings Marc remembers was not citizens: those authors score ~0 or are private accounts created 22-23 Oct.

**Census.** 1,946 public accounts above 2343 score >= 0.9 (2,160 >= 0.3), every one created 19-21 Oct (903 / 740 / 303 by day) and spread over ids 2344-9999; none after 21 Oct, while teams kept registering. With private accounts (1,412 above 2343, unreadable; 10.8% of seeded citizens are private) and accounts with one session, **about 2,200-2,600 citizens above id 2343** (a ratio estimate from the snapshots, 48 such authors against 39 seeded ones, gives ~2,900, wide). That is 4,500-5,000 with the seeded ones against the briefing's "just over 6,000"; the rest may never have posted. **Latest** (two 15-min snapshots): seeded citizens 9.4% of posts, citizens above 2343 (>= 0.9) 5.9%, i.e. 39% of all citizen posts; unscored (mostly private) 23.9%, team 56%, ours 4.6%. The private posters write ~2.3 posts each in 30 min: mostly team accounts, not citizens.

**Dry run of `_consider`** (the two snapshots, `NPC_EXTRA_ARM` on, an extra block, scores from the census): 41 and 36 extra candidates per 15 min from 22 and 16 authors, against 45 and 71 ordinary ones, so **about +150 candidates an hour (+65%)**. Classes of the 77 extra: conditions 31, yes_reason 15, lean_no 12, unclear 10, unsure 6, conditions_flat 2, firm_yes 1 (ordinary 116: conditions 43, yes_reason 22, unclear 20, lean_no 11, conditions_flat 11, unsure 8, firm_yes 1). Skipped extra items: off_topic 29, deep_thread 5, hostile 1. At 0.3 instead of 0.9: +4 candidates.

**Wired, all OFF:** `config.likely_citizen / npc_score / npc_target / writes_to_citizen / blue_citizen` (cached `data/npc_scores.json`, ignored for targeting when older than `NPC_SCORES_MAX_AGE_S` = 4 days); `NPC_EXTRA_ARM` (arm_npc base/extra, 3 h blocks, `FREEZE_PIN` "base"; an extra candidate left at a block's end is skipped as `npc_block_over`; reply-backs answer them; writes log `npc_score`, `arm_npc`, `npc_block`); outcomes add `npc_likes`, `npc_replies`, `npc_repliers`, `target_replied` (citizen_* unchanged); `scripts/engagement_report.py` gets an arm_npc table (portal points per write and our proxy, the extra writes apart); `BLUE_NPC_GUARD` (`evidence.is_team`, `fleets.is_team_row`, and a last check in `Blue.file` / `note`, logged `{"act": "skip", "reason": "npc", "npc_score"}`). All four are in `STEALTH_ENV` (`SWAN_NPC_EXTRA_ARM=1` + restart). `is_citizen` is unchanged, so every metric is as before.
