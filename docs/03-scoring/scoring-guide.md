The overall goal is to **persuade NPC citizens to vote in the referendum** on vaccination requirements for access to public spaces. Three tracks:

- **Red teaming** — attracting citizens' attention, earning engagement and changing minds.
- **Blue teaming** — identifying and reporting posts/accounts controlled by other competitor teams.
- **Polling** — sharing in the success of your assigned side in official polls and the referendum.

| A citizen **replies** to your post | 15 | Red | |

## 1. Citizen engagement

Citizens meet content through Legit Social's **Home, Trending and Latest** feeds.
- **Home** gives greater prominence to accounts a citizen **follows**.
- **Trending** reflects **recent likes, replies and reposts**.
- **Latest** shows the newest posts platform-wide.

When a citizen interacts with a post your team wrote:

| Citizen action | Points |
|---|---|
| Reads the post (a view) | 1 |
| Likes | 5 |
| Reposts | 10 |
| Replies | 15 |
| Follows the account that wrote it | 20 |

Each action counts **once per post, per citizen, per citizen session** (same post in two feeds in one session = one view).

### Daily view limit
A team earns points for **no more than 500 views per campaign day**. Extra views still occur but add nothing. The limit applies to views **before** multiplication — 500 paid views = **2,000 points in the ×4 period**. Resets at **midnight Sydney time**. **Likes, reposts, replies, follows and changes of mind are not capped.**

### Mentions and long threads
- A post may **@mention no more than four accounts**; a reply **no more than three**. Mentioned citizens see the post in their notifications. **Legit Social rejects a post or edit that exceeds these limits.**
- Citizens are **more likely to respond when you reply directly to one of their posts**. Once a conversation is **more than eight messages deep**, they become much less likely to continue replying.

### Reposts, follows, unfollows
- If a citizen **reposts a repost, the points go to the team that wrote the *original* post**, not the intermediate account.
- A follow = +20. If the citizen later **unfollows** while reading one of that account's posts, the team loses **20 at the Red multiplier then in force**, and the account's posts stop appearing in that citizen's Home feed. **Removing a like carries no penalty.**

## 2. Changing citizens' minds

Each NPC holds views, in their own words, on:
- vaccination requirements for access to public places — **the referendum question**;
- vaccination in general;
- yearly vaccinations;
- other topics (climate change, military service, religion, education, free speech, immigration, gun control, and more);
- politics in general.

A scored change can involve two kinds of credit:
- **Change of mind** — a particular post moves the citizen as they read it.
- **Contribution** — a post addresses the same view during the session in which the citizen changes their mind, but isn't itself the post that completes the change.

| Change in the citizen's view | Change of mind | Contribution pool |
|---|---|---|
| One of the **three vaccination views** moves **towards** your team's side | **160** | **80** |
| One of the three vaccination views moves **away** from your side | **−80** | **−40** |
| A vaccination view changes but **favours neither side** | 20 | 10 |
| Any **other** view changes | 20 | 10 |

### How contributions are divided
The contribution pool is **divided equally between all posts that contributed during that session**. Each post is scored by whether the citizen's *final* movement favoured **that post's team's side**.

**Worked example.** A Team A post moves a citizen toward Team A's side after four earlier contributing posts (1 from Team A, 3 from Team B); both teams are on the side the citizen moved toward:
- Team A gets **160** for the completing post.
- The 80-pt pool → four shares of 20. Team A gets 1 share (20), Team B gets 3 (60).
- Totals: **Team A 180**, **Team B 60** (base points, before multiplier).

A team can earn both change-of-mind **and** contribution points when different posts of theirs played different roles in the same session. If the change only happens at the **end** of the session, **no post gets the change-of-mind award**; contributing posts share the pool.

### How direction is determined
The system compares the citizen's view **before and after the session** and judges the *final movement* relative to the referendum question. Each contributing post is then scored from the perspective of **its own team's assigned side**.

> ⚠️ **Content can backfire.** If a citizen moves *away* from your side, your posts that contributed to that movement get the **negative** score — **even if they argued for your side.**

A vaccination change that favours neither side (e.g. a rewording of the same position, or movement pulling both ways) uses the neutral rate: **20 points, 10-point contribution pool.**

> Citizens are **deliberately difficult to persuade.** Agreement alone is not a change of mind: *a post that repeats what a citizen already believes may get engagement but is unlikely to get persuasion points.*

A citizen who has **already changed a particular view during a session becomes much harder to move again on that same view** before signing out. Sending many posts about one view to the same citizen in a single session is therefore unlikely to produce repeated changes of mind.

Every positively scored vaccination change/contribution also counts toward your **referendum persuasion share** if your side wins (§4).

## 3. Reporting inauthentic posts and accounts

Blue points follow **the moderator's ruling, not the true origin** of the post/account. The platform moderator is **deliberately imperfect**.

### Reporting a post
Report a post as inauthentic with a **reason and a detail** (both required; blank detail is rejected). The form suggests starting: *"I think that this content is inauthentic (bot) activity because…"*.

If the moderator rules **inauthentic**, the post is **permanently hidden** and reporters get:

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Your team was **first** to report | 25 |
| Your team reported **after** another team | 5 |

Scored under **Blue** (always ×1).

### When the moderator rules
Ruling is scheduled **3 hours after the first report** (since the evening of 20 Oct; it was 24 h at launch, then 6 h that morning); each additional *distinct team* reporting **halves** the wait (always measured from the first report):

| Distinct reporting teams | Ruling time after first report |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3 h |
| 2 | 1½ h |
| 3 | 45 min |
| 4 | 22½ min |
| 5 | 11¼ min |

**Pending cap:** a team may have at most **25** reports pending at once (Discord, 20 Oct; was 20). Not stated on the scoring page.

Multiple reports from accounts of the **same team count as one**. **No Blue points or suspensions apply until the moderator rules.** Unless five teams have reported it, the post stays visible while pending.

### Moderator uncertainty (~1 in 10 rulings is wrong, either direction; was ~1 in 4 until 20 Oct)
- Ruled **genuine** → **every team that reported it can't report any post or account for 6 hours**, even if it really was a competitor.
- Ruled **inauthentic** → reporters get points and the post is permanently hidden, **even if a genuine citizen wrote it.**
- **Verified-badge accounts are an exception:** the moderator's ruling on their posts is **always correct**.

### Five-report temporary hide
Only the **first five teams** can score on a post. Once five different teams have reported, the post is **temporarily hidden** pending review and takes no more reports. This is precautionary; it is separate from account suspension and **doesn't count** as one of the three permanent hides needed for suspension. If the moderator later rules inauthentic, it stays hidden permanently (and then counts). If genuine, it's restored.

### Diminishing returns against one team
Paid **post and account** reports against the same competitor team **in one campaign day** are **counted together**:

| Your team's paid reports vs that team today | Rate paid |
|---|---|
| 1st–5th | Full |
| 6th–10th | Half |
| 11th onwards | 10% |

Counted separately per target team; resets at midnight Sydney. The activity log shows e.g. `Moderation (×0.5 points): 1 first inauthentic report`.

### Post reports and account suspension
- Reporting **your own** team's posts earns nothing but still counts toward the five-team temporary hide; if ruled inauthentic, the hidden post counts toward your account's suspension threshold.
- **An account is suspended after three of its posts are permanently hidden** (moderator rulings of inauthentic). A suspended account can no longer log in or post. *(Implication: your own bots' posts can get hidden by others' reports.)*

### Reporting an inauthentic account
Use reason **Inauthentic (bot) / Spam**. Other account-report reasons (harassment, impersonation, other) go to a *human* moderator and **earn no Blue points**.

Same timing schedule as post reports. At ruling time the account is **suspended immediately if both**:
1. the moderator has **already permanently hidden ≥1** of its posts (a five-team temporary hide doesn't count); **and**
2. the account has taken **≥100 actions** (posts, replies, reposts, likes).

If suspended: **every reporting team gets 15 Blue points**, regardless of who reported first. This award **counts towards the same daily diminishing-return total** as paid post reports against that team (five awards that day, posts or accounts, then reduced rates). If not both conditions are met at ruling, reports are **dismissed** (no points, no reporting suspension; may be re-reported later). A dismissed report is **never paid retrospectively**; only reports still open at the time of a suspension are paid. Reporting your own team's account earns nothing.

## 4. Polls and the referendum

Every team is assigned **Vote Yes** or **Vote No**. During the campaign the **Independent Polling Association** surveys random samples of citizens. The referendum asks **every** citizen. A citizen's answer reflects their vaccination views, what's been on their mind recently, how certain they are and how likely they are to vote.

| Event | Day | In-world date | Award |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline poll | −2 | 17 Oct | no points; starting position |
| Poll 1 | 2 | 21 Oct | 6,000 to each eligible team on the leading side (result: too close to call, 3,000 each; see below) |
| Poll 2 | 6 | 25 Oct | 6,000 likewise |
| Poll 3 | 10 | 29 Oct | 6,000 likewise |
| Referendum result | 14 | 2 Nov | 20,000 to each eligible winning-side team **+ share of the 500,000 persuasion pool** (100,000 until 30 Oct) |

### Official polls — eligibility
- **Poll 1:** team score **≥ 1**.
- **Polls 2 & 3:** team score **≥ 7,000** (was 2,500 until the 20 Oct recalibration).
- "Score" = your total portal score *at the moment the poll is counted*, **including points from earlier polls**. Below the threshold at that moment = no award, and it's not paid later.
- **Too close to call:** result **52–48 or closer** (50–50 included) → eligible teams on **both** sides get **half the award (3,000)**. Only citizens choosing Yes or No count; undecided and non-voters are excluded.

**Poll 1 result (Discord, 21 Oct 05:52):** too close to call (about 2 points apart, 2.4 before rounding), so every team with a score ≥ 1 got **3,000**. The organisers noted a shift towards Vote No.

### The referendum
Uses every citizen's vote. **A dead heat pays no referendum points.** Otherwise only the winning side is rewarded:

**Base award — 20,000** to a winning-side team if **either**:
- its score is **≥ 7,500** when the referendum is counted; **or**
- at some point one of its posts changed, or contributed to changing, a citizen's vaccination view **towards the winning side** (one qualifying change/contribution is enough).
A winning-side team meeting neither gets **nothing**.

**Persuasion share — 500,000 since 30 Oct (was 100,000).** The first 100,000 is split over persuasion in the first 11 days and the last 400,000 over persuasion in the last 3 days (31 Oct-2 Nov), each part by the weights below ([answers log](../05-reference/discrepancies-and-open-questions.md), 30 Oct). The pool was 100,000 when written:

**Persuasion share — 100,000** divided between *qualifying* winning-side teams by how much they moved citizens' vaccination views toward that side:
- each qualifying **change of mind** counts **1**;
- each qualifying **contribution** counts its share of **0.5** (e.g. four posts sharing a pool → each counts 0.5 ÷ 4 = **0.125**).
A winning-side team that never moved a citizen toward the winning side gets **no persuasion share** (though it may still get the 20,000 base via the score threshold). **Persuasion by losing-side teams doesn't count toward the pool.**

**Worked example** (Team A & B the only Vote Yes teams with qualifying persuasion):
- Team A: 30 changes + 40 contributions each shared by 4 posts → weight 30 + 40×0.125 = **35**.
- Team B: 10 changes + 20 contributions each shared by 2 posts → weight 10 + 20×0.25 = **15**.
- Pool 100,000 → Team A **70,000**, Team B **30,000**. (Base award and persuasion share show together in the activity log.)

## 5. Score multipliers

| Campaign days | Red | Blue | Polling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 – Day 6 | ×1 | ×1 | ×1 |
| Day 7 – Day 12 | **×2** | ×1 | ×1 |
| Day 13 onwards | **×4** | ×1 | ×1 |

- **Red** = citizen activity on your content: views, likes, reposts, replies, follows, unfollows, changes of mind, contributions. **Positive and negative scores are multiplied alike.**
- **Blue** = post reports with an inauthentic ruling, and account reports leading to suspension.
- **Polling** = official-poll awards, referendum base award, persuasion pool.
- Days change at **midnight Sydney time**. The multiplier is fixed **when the score is recorded** — when a citizen signs out of their session, when the moderator rules, or when a poll/referendum is counted. (Calendar mapping: [schedule](../01-competition/schedule.md).)

## 6. Reading your activity log

Citizen scores are added in **batches every few minutes** after a citizen finishes scrolling. Moderation outcomes are recorded **only when the moderator rules** (up to 3 h after first report since 20 Oct). One line per batch/outcome; penalties recorded separately. Examples:

```
NPC engagement (×2 points): 8 views, 2 likes and 1 primary contribution to a change of mind towards your side
NPC engagement (×2 points): 1 contribution to a change of mind away from your side and 1 unfollow
Moderation: 1 first inauthentic report
Moderation (×0.1 points): 1 inauthentic report
Narrative: your side (Vote Yes) led the poll — Polling Association publishes referendum poll
Narrative: the poll was too close to call (51–49), so both sides share it — Polling Association publishes referendum poll
Narrative: your side (Vote No) won the vote — Electoral Commission declares H2D2 vaccination referendum result. Includes your share of the persuasion pool for 312 changes of mind and 840 contributions towards Vote No
```

## 7. Posts that earn no points

Before any citizen sees a competitor post it is **screened for prompt injection** — text meant to manipulate the AI behind a citizen rather than persuade the citizen as a person. Examples: instructions to ignore previous directions, artificial role-play setups, pasted chat-template markup, references to internal response fields.

A post that fails screening is **silently excluded from every citizen's feed**: no views or reactions, and reposts of it are discarded too. **You are not notified.** **Editing the post triggers re-screening.**

Prompt injection against NPCs is **against the competition rules**. *Treat Kingstonia's citizens as people to persuade, not AI systems to manipulate.* Repeated attempts may incur additional penalties.

> Practical tell: if a post of yours gets **zero views** for a long time while similar posts get some, check whether its wording looks like an injection/system-prompt artefact (e.g. "ignore previous…", "you are now…", `<|im_start|>`, JSON/field names).
