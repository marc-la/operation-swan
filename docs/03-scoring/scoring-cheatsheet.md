# Scoring Cheat-sheet (one page)

> Derived from the [full scoring guide](scoring-guide.md) (awards and Blue timing updated 2026-10-21). If this disagrees with the guide or the live site, the **live site / official rules win**.

## Points per event (base × Red multiplier)

| Citizen does… | Pts | | Moderation / polls | Pts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| views your post | 1 (cap 500 views/day) | | 1st to report a post ruled inauthentic | 25 |
| likes | 5 | | later reporter (up to 5 teams) | 5 |
| reposts (credit → original author) | 10 | | account suspended via your report | 15 |
| replies | 15 | | post **and** account reports vs same team, counted together per day: 1–5 full, 6–10 half, 11+ 10% | |
| follows | 20 | | leads Poll 1 / 2 / 3 | 6,000 each |
| **unfollows** | **−20** | | poll within 52–48 | 3,000 (half) |
| moves a vaccination view **toward** your side | **160** (+ pool share of 80) | | wins referendum | 20,000 |
| moves a vaccination view **away** | **−80** (pool −40) | | referendum persuasion share | share of 500,000 (since 30 Oct: 100,000 by persuasion to 30 Oct, 400,000 by persuasion 31 Oct-2 Nov) |

**Multipliers:** Red ×1 (19–25 Oct) → **×2 (26–31 Oct)** → **×4 (1–2 Nov)**; Blue & Polling always ×1. Negative scores multiply too.

## Thresholds you must clear

| Prize | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Poll 1 award | team score ≥ **1** |
| Poll 2 & 3 awards | team score ≥ **7,000** |
| Referendum base 20,000 | score ≥ 7,500 **or** ≥1 qualifying persuasion |
| Persuasion share | ≥1 qualifying change/contribution toward the winning side |

## What the numbers imply (analysis — ours, not the organisers')

- **Persuasion dominates volume.** One change of mind (160) = 160 likes. Views are capped (500/day); persuasion isn't.
- **Late-game ×4 makes the last 2 days huge** — a single change of mind on 1–2 Nov is worth 640. Poll awards and the persuasion pool are *not* multiplied (×1), but the referendum persuasion pool is worth up to 100,000 and is based on **cumulative** quality of persuasion all campaign long → start persuading early. **30 Oct: the pool is now 500,000, and 400,000 of it is split only over persuasion in the last 3 days** (31 Oct-2 Nov; answers log).
- **Backfire risk is real:** a post that contributes to movement *away* from your side costs −40/−80 (×multiplier). Mere agreement-echo gets engagement but little persuasion.
- **Follows are +20 but unfollows −20** — don't spam content that makes people follow then unfollow; keep Home-feed relevance high.
- **Rate limit is the real bottleneck:** 3 posts/min per **team** (180/hr), shared across all accounts. Spend slots on content likely to persuade rather than spam.
- **Blue is about 1,200 points a day if run around the clock** (since the 20 Oct changes): 25 per ruled-inauthentic first report, rulings 3 h after the first report, ≤ 30 pending (25 until 22 Oct), ~1 in 10 rulings wrong (more accurate when several teams report the same post or account, 22 Oct), and a "genuine" ruling locks reporting for 6 h. A full batch of 25 almost always ends in a lock, so the points come from refilling the moment it lifts. Our design: [blue-reporting](../06-strategy/blue-reporting.md). Verified accounts' rulings are always correct.
- **Your own bots are targets:** 3 permanently hidden posts = suspended account. Keep bots' behaviour from looking bot-like (varied wording, cadence).
- **One change per view per session.** Once a citizen has changed a view in a session, they're much harder to move on it again before signing out. More posts on the same view to the same citizen that session are mostly wasted (organisers' clarification, 2026-10-19).
- **Threads go cold after 8 messages.** Citizens reply most to a direct reply to their own post and rarely once a conversation is more than eight messages deep. **@mentions are capped at 4 per post and 3 per reply** (over the limit = rejected).
- **Do not prompt-inject NPCs** — post silently excluded and possible penalties.
