## Provenance & accuracy

- The pasted `.txt` files were compared **word-for-word** against the live pages on 2026-10-19: the text matched. What the pastes lacked was the **images/screenshots, link targets, the PIS PDF, the 2025 and privacy pages**, and formatting — all added here.
- Rules, scoring, setting and getting-started text is reproduced faithfully (layout cleaned). Lines marked *Note*, **(derived)**, "analysis", or in `discrepancies-and-open-questions.md` are **ours**, not the organisers'.
- **Organisers may change rules/scoring/rate limits during the competition** (they say so). Re-check the live pages and Discord announcements; when something changes, update the relevant file and add the date.
- Not captured: the Discord server contents, the community portal behind login (team page, account caps, leaderboard), and the Kingstonia flag / dolphin-flu art (linked, not mirrored).

## Maintaining this

- One topic per file; link, don't duplicate. Put new organiser rulings in the **Answers** table of the discrepancies file.
- Date-stamp changes ("updated 2026-10-2x: organisers raised the post limit to …").
- Never put the team code, passwords or tokens in docs.
