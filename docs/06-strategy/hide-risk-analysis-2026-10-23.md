Question (Marc, 23 Oct): after seven voices were "instantly banned" when the parked ones were released, what drives hides and suspensions, which variables are ours to change, and what does stealth cost in conversion? Five read-only analyses of the box's logs (668k action rows, 66 labelled hides, 30 writing voices, 4 days). Raw reports, each with method, tables and script list: [research-2026-10-23/](research-2026-10-23/) (`accounts`, `content`, `voices`, `adversary`, `conversion`; shared `BRIEF.md`). Earlier audits: [stealth.md](stealth.md), [hidden-post-audit-2026-10-23.md](hidden-post-audit-2026-10-23.md).

## The answer in ten lines
1. **The release did not ban the seven.** The portal's own ban log shows malakai_r94 (22 Oct 22:34) and ellaleota (23 Oct 09:44) were banned *before* the 10:24 restart; the bot only learns of a ban by a 403 on its next action (11.9 h and 40 min late). The other five were "3 posts hidden" bans whose third post was written 3.0–3.4 h earlier, before the release. Only 3 of the 7 were parked at all.
2. **Parking is unproven, and a stop at 2 hides cannot work.** All three bad posts were already written before the second hide showed. A stop at the *first* visible hide would have saved about 10 of the 14 three-hide bans (analyst estimate). The four later parked survivors are confounded with calendar time.
3. **The true hide rate is 1.3–1.5% of text writes, not 0.9%.** 66 hides in 4,664 uncensored writes = 1.37% [1.08, 1.75]; the last 24 h is 1.40%. The 0.9% appears only when the 553 booster reposts are in the denominator. It was 0.36% before 21 Oct 12:00 and 1.5% after, when rivals' reporting started. At the post-fix rate 0.6% is unlikely (P = 0.009) and **0.3% is out of reach of any content change seen so far.**
4. **A voice dies at about 145 writes (bootstrap 127–178), not at an age.** Hides are proportional to writes (doubling a voice-day's writes: RR 0.91 per write), so lifetime in hours is set by write rate. Median time from the first visible hide to ban is 11 h; from the second, 3 h.
5. **Profile and identity do not explain it.** After controlling for time, per-write hide rates do not differ between voices (p = 0.40–0.50). 62 features across 29 voices (role, age, place, handle, display name, original vs converted, card re-used from a banned voice, pace, hours active, second voices) leave nothing after multiple-testing correction (smallest q = 0.84). Only effects above about 3x are excluded.
6. **One real structural effect:** a converted booster's first top-level posts are hidden 5 of 15 times (33%) against 1 of 122 for original voices (Fisher p = 4.5e-5); their replies are normal. Found independently by `voices` and `adversary`. Cause unknown (the boosters' earlier like-ring history is the suspect).
7. **Text matters modestly, not decisively.** A reporter's-eye scorer built from public features does about as well as chance on later data (AUC 0.37–0.56). Effects measured within a 6 h window are real but small (table below). 15% of writes hold 54% of hides.
8. **Hides arrive in batches** (29 of 55 have another voice's hide within ±3 min vs 8 by chance), and the batches are shrinking (7, 4, 2 clusters over three eras), so single hides now dominate. Rival behaviour is the control we cannot move.
9. **Conversion: persuasion is about 2% of our points and nets to roughly zero; engagement is 88%.** The 41 "away" items in 48 h are 39 pool-share contributions worth about -3 each plus 2 of our own -80 hits (one burst, 23 Oct 09:00–11:00). The three tells carried no measurable conversion or engagement, so removing them was free. Longevity earns nothing; content volume does (about 1.2–2.0 pts per write at the margin).
10. **The binding constraint is the account allowance.** About 70 registered, 130 left (portal count). At roughly writes-per-day ÷ 145 deaths a day (3,000 writes → up to ~20), the spare accounts last about 4–10 days against 10 days remaining. Halving the death rate buys days; this is where stealth pays in points (a dead voice costs about 100–600 pts to replace, a hide about 35–200).

**Checked and flat** (no need to spend effort): the 40-nursing-posts fact and the venue-list/end-date/"binds" skeletons; similarity to own history or other voices; backend; spelling hand; handle shape and display name; card role/place/age; original vs converted (except top-level posts); second voices (0.45%, the safest write); more concurrent voices (dilution RR 1.01); being the busiest voice of the hour. A voice's per-write risk does *not* rise after its first hide (1.58% vs 1.60%); the reason to rest it is the account-report route, not a higher hide rate.

**Where the analyses disagree or are thin.** `content` finds adjusted effects of RR ~2 on specific phrases; `adversary` finds text does not predict hides on a time holdout. These fit together: at a 1.4% base rate an RR of 2 gives AUC ~0.55, and effects such as contractions and "voting No" flipped between time halves as prompts changed. Treat text levers as a modest, regime-dependent gain (content estimates 1.28% → ~1.0%), not as a route to 0.3%. The "0.12% remainder" in `content` is in-sample and snooped; do not quote it. We cannot see reports, who reports, or rival-bot traffic, so nothing here measures "blending in with embassy traffic".

## Answers to the specific questions
- **Do longer-lived accounts with more content earn more points?** Volume yes (linearly, ~1.3 pts per write early, ~0.8 now), longevity no: the scoring guide has no age term and a per-post regression with calendar-time controls gives +0.06 pts/day of age [-0.20, +0.32]. Citizen followers (+20 each) accrue slowly with age, about 5% of points. Voices that look more productive are simply the early ones, when yield per post was 2.65 (now 0.80).
- **Does parking make an account less likely to be banned?** Not shown. Silent vs writing hazard after the second hide: 0.60 vs 1.41 per 10 voice-h, RR 2.3 with CI 0.7–12. Account-route bans hit silent voices (4 of 7). The early parked cohort died 10 of 10; the later four survivors are confounded with the account route stopping after 22 Oct 07:45.
- **If we push the pool, do we just produce the same accounts to be banned?** New persona cards will not be riskier or safer per write on any measured profile field, so pushing the pool does not worsen the rate. It does not improve it either. Put the first-24-hours rule in first; the pool only multiplies deaths unless the per-voice write budget is set against the allowance.
- **Is moving-away a natural consequence of messaging a soft Yes?** Mostly it is not a meaningful loss. 61% of contacted citizens are "yes, but" soft Yes; 39 of 41 away items are shared pool contributions at about -3. Our own -80 hits were 2, in one 2 h burst, against 1 own +160. Away per write rose 2.5x after the tells came out (90% CI 1.4–4.4; the cause was not found and the tells do not explain it) and toward rose 2.3x with it; cause unknown. No stance group has negative expected value, so there is nothing to save by skipping any. **The pool is nearly used up:** 1,384 of 2,332 citizens contacted; first contacts fell from 52% to 21% of writes, and 51% of writes go to citizens already contacted 4+ times (a repeat still earns 70–85% of a first contact). That, more than tone, is the conversion problem, and the repeat-contact ring signature is a stealth cost.
- **What is the right acceptable hide rate?** On this evidence 1.0% is a stretch target for content levers; 0.6% needs rival reporting to ease or fewer writes per voice; 0.3% was only seen before rivals started reporting (0.36%).

## The risk-aware de-risking loop (your automation idea)
Spec is in [adversary.md §8](research-2026-10-23/adversary.md): regularised logistic with voice and 6 h-block fixed effects on a rolling 72 h window (a deploy resets the evidence); amendments only when a shrunk RR ≥ 1.5 replicates in two non-overlapping time halves, max 2 per card, written to `derisk.jsonl`; hooks `src/swan_bot/derisk.py`, `DERISK_REPORT` on, **`DERISK_APPLY` off**, called from `pool.py` when a card is re-queued. **Honest read of the data:** profile-level de-risking has no detectable payoff today, and the only large profile-level effect (converted first posts) is a fixed rule, not something to learn. So build it as a report-only monitor first, wired to the logging in item 7, and let it propose amendments only after about 15 hides under one prompt regime and a randomised 50/50 per voice-day to measure them. Draft message for the pool-automation session (not sent): "Pool: add (a) a min-writes gate so converted/new voices make no top-level post/thread/amplified post in their first 24 h; (b) read `account_log.suspended` from the portal on a timer to mark status; (c) per-hide logging (suspension source/time, card attributes); (d) a report-only `derisk.py` per adversary.md §8, `DERISK_APPLY` off. Profile attributes (role, handle, age, place) show no measurable hide effect, so do not pick cards for risk."

## Caveats that apply to everything above
One team, four days, 66 hides, hides clustered in sweeps; every estimate shares one rival regime that changed on 21 Oct 12:00. Hide labels are a union of watch, alerts and the lab corpus; after a voice is suspended the watch stops looking, so per-voice counts are lower bounds (small in aggregate: 1.37% → 1.33%). We observe being ruled hidden, not being reported. `sv_delay` and the converted-first-post effect have no known mechanism. R (the cost of a dead voice) is the key unknown for the stealth-versus-volume trade; the conversion analyst proposes a 10% hold-out of citizen ids to measure drift.
