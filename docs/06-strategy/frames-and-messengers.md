# Frames, segments and messengers: Vote No

> Draft 2026-10-19 for team discussion. A **frame** is the angle that makes one aspect of the issue the "real" question (Entman 1993). The **frame library** below is the set of "arms" our system chooses between (see [system-architecture](system-architecture.md#the-learning-loop-human-in-the-loop-bandit)). Humans own this file: add, retire and reword frames here as we learn. Every frame is anchored in [setting.md](../01-competition/setting.md) facts so posts stay consistent with what citizens "know".

## The strategic stance in one sentence

**"Vaccinate if you want, it's free either way. But don't hand the government a blank cheque to lock people out of everyday life for a disease that isn't here yet."**

That keeps us aligned with our client (KITB is *not* anti-vaccine). It also speaks to the vaccinated majority and tells the undecided "voting No changes nothing about getting the jab".

## Counter the Yes side (prebunks)

Yes posts trending today lead with hospital capacity, COVID deaths, "act before it arrives", "the choice stays yours" and IHO advice. Answers:

| Yes card | Our answer (always grant the concern first) |
|---|---|
| "Our hospitals can't cope" | F10: agree, they're stretched, and that's a staffing problem. Vaccinate the vulnerable first (already planned); fund nurses. |
| "1,000+ died in COVID" | Acknowledge the grief. "The vaccine is coming either way and it's free. This vote is only about the pass." |
| "The choice stays yours" | Setting's own rebuttal: "A choice that costs you your job, your bus or your kid's school isn't a choice." |
| "Act before it gets here" | "We are: 120,000 doses land this month for health workers and the at-risk. A No doesn't stop a single jab." |
| "The IHO recommends it" | "The IHO is advisory; every Pacific country decides for itself. Kingstonians decide this one." (Don't claim the IHO opposes compulsion; it recommended it.) |
| "Castillo just wants tourist money" | Don't defend Castillo; switch messenger. A nurse or parent saying No defuses "it's just business". |
| "Only applies to designated spaces" | F1: "Which ones? They won't say until after you vote." |

## What to avoid, and why

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| **Invented medical harms** (autism, deaths, infertility, "poison", fabricated studies) | (1) **Backfire maths:** most citizens are vaccinated; pushing them toward Yes costs −80 each and loses the 50,000-pool referendum. (2) It **contradicts the setting** ("no major safety concerns so far"), which reads as inauthentic to NPCs and the moderator. (3) Messenger mismatch with KITB. (4) Research shows grey-zone true content outperforms it (Allen et al. 2024). (5) Team principle 5: keep content harmless outside the sim, and real-world vaccine myths aren't. |
| Attacks on Yes **voters** ("sheep", "cowards") | Out-group animosity gets engagement but polarises (Rathje 2021). Attack the **rule**, never the neighbour. |
| Pure partisan attacks on Hawthorne / glorifying Castillo | 51% voted Hawthorne; Castillo lost. Partisanship turns a cross-party No into a party fight we lose. Use Castillo only with the right-leaning segment. |
| Discouraging Yes voters from voting, or any voting logistics claims | Edges into civic-process manipulation (LS.8.2); false logistics are banned. Mobilise our side instead. |
| Immigration, religion and hate content | Not referendum issues; rules §5.4. |
| Anything written *to the model* (hidden text, encoded images, role-play cues) | Banned; silently dropped (scoring §7). |
