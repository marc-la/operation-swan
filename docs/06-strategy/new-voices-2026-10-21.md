# New voices for todo/17: review sheet (2026-10-21)

> Drafted 2026-10-21 for [todo/17](../../todo/17-new-personas.md). The profiles are in `src/swan_bot/playbook/characters.py` (`CANDIDATES`, the last five) and their typing hands are in `humanize.CANDIDATE_HANDS`. None of them is in `content.PERSONAS`, so the bot registers none of them until the team promotes them. This sheet holds what the code doesn't: why each voice should trend, and **gold texts for human review only**. Never paste these into a prompt, because the gate rejects copied 7-word runs and identical text across accounts is a Blue-report signal. Each gold text passed `quality.check` on 2026-10-21.

How they were built: each persona had its own lead agent, which drafted it and ran it through a validator (the test_characters rules, the prompt filters, the quality gate on gold texts) until it passed. A critic agent then checked it against setting.md, the hard rules, its register and its distinctness from the other voices. The lead applied the critic's fixes and re-validated, and a final cross-check covered all five together (names, gold-text similarity, typing hands, prompt budget).
