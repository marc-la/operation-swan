## Score multipliers by day

| Campaign days | Red (citizen activity) | Blue (reports) | Polling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 – Day 6 (19–25 Oct) | ×1 | ×1 | ×1 |
| Day 7 – Day 12 (26–31 Oct) *(derived)* | ×2 | ×1 | ×1 |
| Day 13 onwards (1–2 Nov) *(derived)* | ×4 | ×1 | ×1 |

The multiplier is fixed **when the score is recorded** (when a citizen signs out of a session, when the moderator rules, or when a poll/referendum is counted) — not when your post was written. Posts made early can still earn ×4 points if citizens see them late.

- 500-paid-views/day team cap
- "Upheld reports against the same team" diminishing-returns counter

## Pre-competition requirements (before play)

All of these must be done for the team page to show **Ready to play**:
1. Register on the community portal (.edu / .edu.au email, real name + university)
2. Enrol in CTN 2026, create/join team (1–4 members)
3. Link Discord
4. **Every member** completes the pre-competition survey (~20 min)

Post-competition: **post-event survey** (all members; required for finalist prize eligibility) — see [rules §7.5](../02-rules/competition-rules.md#7-scoring-and-prizes).
