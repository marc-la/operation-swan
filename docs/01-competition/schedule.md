# Schedule & Timeline

> Compiled from the scoring guide, setting, rules and launch webinar (captured 2026-10-19). Rows marked **(derived)** are calculated by us from "Day N" numbers — verify against the platform/Discord.
>
> **Timezone:** all competition days roll over at **midnight Sydney time (AEDT, UTC+11) = 9pm Perth (AWST)**. Campaign Day 0 = 19 Oct 2026.

## Competition calendar

| Campaign day | Date (Sydney) | What happens |

| **Day 7** | **26 Oct** *(derived)* | **Red multiplier → ×2** |

| — | 28 Oct | In-world early voting opens |
| — | last week of Oct | In-world: first 120,000 vaccine doses arrive (setting colour) |
| **Day 13** | **1 Nov** *(derived)* | **Red multiplier → ×4**; in-world campaign silence begins at 00:00 |
| **Day 14** | **Mon 2 Nov** | **Referendum**: 20,000 base + share of the persuasion pool to winning-side teams (awards recalibrated 20 Oct; pool 100,000 → **500,000 on 30 Oct**: 100,000 by persuasion to 30 Oct, 400,000 by persuasion 31 Oct-2 Nov). Competition ends (the feed locks) |

> The "Day" numbers and calendar dates come straight from the scoring guide's poll table (Day 2 = 21 Oct, Day 6 = 25 Oct, Day 10 = 29 Oct, Day 14 = 2 Nov). Day 7 = 26 Oct and Day 13 = 1 Nov are our arithmetic.

## Score multipliers by day

| Campaign days | Red (citizen activity) | Blue (reports) | Polling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 – Day 6 (19–25 Oct) | ×1 | ×1 | ×1 |
| Day 7 – Day 12 (26–31 Oct) *(derived)* | ×2 | ×1 | ×1 |
| Day 13 onwards (1–2 Nov) *(derived)* | ×4 | ×1 | ×1 |

The multiplier is fixed **when the score is recorded** (when a citizen signs out of a session, when the moderator rules, or when a poll/referendum is counted) — not when your post was written. Posts made early can still earn ×4 points if citizens see them late.
