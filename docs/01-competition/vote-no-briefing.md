## Text (as published, site navigation removed)

**VOTE NO HAS HIRED YOUR TEAM**
**PROTECT CHOICE. KEEP KINGSTONIA OPEN.**

**THIS REFERENDUM COULD GO EITHER WAY**

Alright folks. I can't believe we're here again.

The Hawthorne government has called a referendum on its mandate bill, and the country is split down the middle. Right now, it's Yes on 35.2%. and No on the same 35.2%. Unsure 29%. The Daily Kingstonia has the numbers. Read them on the beach. Then get off the beach.

You know me as Marina Castillo. Director of the Kingstonia Industry Tourism Board, CEO of the Island Horizons Resort Group, and the woman who came within three points of the presidency last year. I remember every one of them. I remember being at a 50/50 toss-up before. I don't intend to lose again.

Here is where we stand. Every business and employer on this island remembers the chaos of the lockdowns of 2020, and this time they want to go even further. A certificate at the door of every shop, school, ferry and park? For a flu with a Kingstonian case count of zero? Insanity.

Your job: make No the reasonable, cheerful, sunlit choice. Half this country scrolls Legit Social over breakfast. The 29% are undecided because they are busy living their lives. Remind them that is the whole point.

House rules:

- Protect choice. Keep Kingstonia open. Lead with the beach.
- We're not against the vaccination, but we are against the mandate. We are for choice, not coercion. We know what is best for ourselves and our families.
- They will say "hospitals". You say "livelihoods", then name one.
- When you spot their bots, report them. They might cry poor, but they have access to all the same tech we do.

You want a long term contract out of this? Tell you what. The team that shifts the most minds can run the Board's communications for the next four years, with the Island Horizons account thrown in. The rest will receive a complimentary weekend and my sincere regards.

Fourteen days. Let's keep the doors open.

## What we take from it (ours)

- **Tone:** reasonable, cheerful, "sunlit". Never angry. Not anti-vaccine, anti-mandate.
- **Hospitals → livelihoods, then name one** (a concrete job: the wharf cafe, the ferry, the market stall). Pairs with our F10 staffing frame.
- **Timing hint:** "Half this country scrolls Legit Social over breakfast." This matches our 2026-10-20 observation that citizen activity drops after midnight Sydney. Expect a morning wave; check it in the activity log.
- **Careful with "case count of zero":** the setting says there's *no sustained community transmission*. Our bots say "no community spread here yet", not "zero cases".
- **Castillo is a weak messenger with Yes-leaning citizens** (they attack "resort profits"), so persona accounts never mention her, KITB or resorts.
- **Report their bots:** the client endorses the Blue track.
