## B. Open questions to put to organisers

1. Does the **campaign-day multiplier** timeline match our derivation (×2 from 26 Oct, ×4 from 1 Nov)?
2. Is **editing a post** to re-screen it risk-free, i.e. no penalty for having tripped the injection screen?
3. Are **verified accounts** organiser-controlled personas (public figures, journalists)? Can a team account get verified?
4. Is it within the rules for our own accounts to **like or repost our own posts** (in-game LS.5.3 calls this "reaction farming")? Is the only consequence in-game (reports, hides)? See [scoring-roi](../06-strategy/scoring-roi.md#legitimate-edges-confirm-the-asterisked-ones).
5. May we use an **external LLM for analysis** (classifying the stance and topics of posts we read), or only for content generation (§5.1 vs §6.1)? *Decided internally 2026-10-22 (Marc): yes for offline analysis and testing that never touches the platform. §5.1 allows "personal machines or cloud services to develop and test" and §6.1 allows AI for "data analysis". CLAUDE.md rule 2 updated. Still worth an organiser confirmation in `#questions`.*
6. May we **automate reading our own portal activity log**, or should it be read manually?
7. Does the in-world **campaign silence** (00:00 1 Nov: paid ads, mass campaign messaging and rallies restricted) change how NPCs, the moderator or scoring treat campaign-style posts during the ×4 days?
8. Do NPCs perceive **GIF/image links** or the `embed` field, or only text?
9. What counts as "unique engagements" for the tie-break (§7.7)?
10. **Blue, daily diminishing rate:** is an award counted on the Sydney day the report was **filed** or the day it was **ruled**? A report filed at 8:30pm Perth rules after midnight Sydney. The scoring page says only "The count resets at midnight Sydney time", but it fixes the multiplier "when the moderator rules", so `swan_blue` assumes the ruling day. (Not yet asked.)
11. **Blue, pending cap:** do **account** reports count toward the pending-report cap (30 since 22 Oct)? *Observed yes, 22 Oct (answers log); not confirmed by organisers.* (Not yet asked.)
12. **Early voting (2026-10-27):** the setting page says in-world early voting opens 28 Oct. Does an early vote fix that citizen's referendum answer, or is every answer taken at the 2 Nov count (scoring §4: "what's been on their mind recently, how certain they are and how likely they are to vote")? (Not yet asked.)
13. **Certainty and turnout (2026-10-27):** can a change in how certain a citizen is, or how likely they are to vote, score as a change of mind (towards, or neither side), or does it only feed the count? Scoring §2 lists the three vaccination views and other topics; neither is listed. (Not yet asked.)
14. **Citizens added mid-game (2026-10-27):** have NPC citizens been added since launch, and how many are there now ("just over 6,000"; we recognise 2,332, ids 12-2343)? Ids 4400-4790 were created 19 Oct 17:00-20:53 UTC one every ~36 s with citizen-style names and posting rhythm ([targeting-audit](../06-strategy/targeting-audit-2026-10-27.md)). 29 Oct: the portal books follows of our accounts by the 2,268 accounts above 2343 that score 0.9+ as citizen follows (25 of them since 23 Oct; rival bots' 508 are not booked), so they behave as citizens in scoring ([situation-2026-10-29 §4](../06-strategy/situation-2026-10-29.md#4-the-missing-citizens-the-biggest-lever)). Draft wording there (§11). (Not yet asked.)
