# Privacy Policy (summary)

> Source: https://capturethenarrative.com/info/privacy-policy (effective **31 August 2026**; captured 2026-10-19 via summary — read the full page for legal detail).

- **Operator:** Digital Lies and Cyber Literacy Foundation Limited (ABN 32 700 329 586), trading as DLCL. References the Australian Privacy Act 1988 / APPs.
- **Collected:** name, academic email, institution, student ID, country, login details, referral code, consent records; enrolments, teams, roles, referrals, linked accounts (e.g. Discord), messages, submissions, scores, rankings, awards, moderation records, prize info; IP address, device/browser details, cookies, session IDs, timestamps, security logs; optional public social handles/posts you provide for referrals/publicity.
- **Retention:** identifiable records kept while your account is active and as needed for administration, awards, security, disputes, legal obligations. You may delete your account (or contact the Privacy Officer); some records may be kept for legal/financial/security/dispute reasons; backups overwritten in normal cycle. **Public results may be kept indefinitely**; team submissions may remain, de-identified where practical.
- **Public leaderboards** may show display/team names, institution, scores, rankings, finalist/winner status. **Academic emails and student IDs are not published.**
- **Research** use of data is separate from administration and needs its own participant information/consent. Declining/withdrawing from research doesn't by itself close your account or disqualify you, unless research materials say otherwise. *(Note rules §4.1 make research consent part of participation — check with organisers if a member is uncomfortable.)*
- **Sharing:** service providers, partners (judges, sponsors, institutions), connected services, authorities where legally required, the public (results only). **Not sold or rented.** May be processed overseas (likely Australia and US).
- **Automated decisions:** eligibility, scoring, ranking and moderation may be made wholly/largely by computer; you can request review.
- **Your rights:** access, correction, deletion, withdraw consent, change communication preferences, complain.
- **Contact:** Privacy Officer **privacy@capturethenarrative.com** (aims to respond to complaints within 30 days); then the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).
