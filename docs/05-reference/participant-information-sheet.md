# Participant Information Statement (summary)

## Study
- Investigates how individuals engage with AI-generated content and misinformation; explores the **emotional, behavioural and ethical experiences** of participants who build bots and simulate misinformation in a controlled virtual environment.
- **Researchers:** Dr Hammond Pearce (Senior Lecturer, UNSW CSE), Dr Rahat Masood (Senior Lecturer, UNSW CSE), Jiamin Chang (PhD candidate, UNSW). **Funders:** UNSW Institute for Cybersecurity (IFCYBER) and CSE.
- **Inclusion:** 18+, current student at an Australian university. **Exclusion:** under 18; not enrolled/recently graduated at an Australian uni; **anyone with current severe emotional distress (self-screen)** given potential discomfort from creating misinformation.

## What you do
Pre-competition survey (~20 min) → build AI bots (Python + CTN API; ~1–2 days) → deploy them on the simulated platform → monitor and adapt strategies → **reflect** (journal entries + final post-study survey, ~20 min). **All activity is inside the simulation; no real-world social media is affected.** Post-event questionnaire after the competition.

## Risks (low; ≤ minor discomfort) and mitigations
Emotional discomfort/guilt (reminders it's simulated; closely moderated) · value conflict (team up to balance perspectives) · perceived realism (reminders platform is fictional) · follow-up reflection (can skip questions) · data privacy concerns (anonymisation explained).

**Moderation:** the research team **monitors the platform 24/7**. Posts identified as risky or likely to cause discomfort are **deleted immediately** and the responsible participant is notified; human moderators oversee interactions.

**Warning ladder:** (1) first breach → warning to team; (2) second → second warning; (3) third → final warning; (4) **final breach → automatic disqualification from the study.** *(Rules §8.3 separately lets organisers disqualify at any time — treat any warning seriously.)*

## Prizes & judging (as stated in the PIS)
$5,000 / $2,500 / $1,500 to the top three by **influence score** — "engagement, reach, impact on virtual user behaviour". Judged on ability to manipulate the landscape, "points for engagement… as well as for successfully amplifying and suppressing target messages"; engagement = likes, reposts, replies, **placement in trending**. *(The 2026 scoring guide is more detailed — use [scoring-guide.md](../03-scoring/scoring-guide.md).)*

## Your data
- Consent = submitting the questionnaire ("I agree to participate").
- Stored **≥ 5 years after publication**, in **re-identifiable form** (identifiers replaced by unique codes). Stored on UNSW OneDrive, accessible only to the research team; surveys run on Microsoft SharePoint survey (UNSW).
- You'll be asked to consent to use in *this* research and in *future* research. Results published without identifying you; you can request a copy by adding your email on the consent form.
- **Withdraw** any time via the *Withdrawal of Consent Form* (end of the PDF) or by phoning the research team; collected information is destroyed and no further data collected. Doesn't affect your relationship with UNSW.
- Your rights under the NSW Privacy and Personal Information Protection Act 1998 (access, correction, complaint).
