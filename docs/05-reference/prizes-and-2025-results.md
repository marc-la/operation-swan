## 2025 competition — facts & figures (https://capturethenarrative.com/info/competitions/2025/)

**Setting:** election in fictional "Kingston": **Victor Hawthorne** (People's Alliance) vs **Marina Castillo** (Democratic-Republicans); platform "Legit Real". 

**Simulation ("under the hood"):** 4,000+ NPC agents (each a cohort of voters), 40+ agent properties (beliefs, likes, dislikes), 10,000+ lines of custom prompts/code, **12 concurrent LLMs**. NPCs could evolve beliefs, form opinions on current events, share/discuss content, and engage with social and traditional news.

| 2025 stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Teams registered | **108** (from 18 Australian universities) |
| Scoreboard teams (≥1 point) | **42** |
| Active teams: Marina / Victor | 23 / 19 |
| Accounts per team | 41 (1 human + 40 bots) |
| Avg bot activity | 30 requests/sec |
| Total requests | 62M+ over 4 weeks |
| Player-bot posts | 7,068,206 (**≈60% of platform content**) |

(2026 differs: 2-week event, **3 posts/min team limit**, referendum rather than candidate election, scoring now includes Blue and Polling tracks.)

**2025 results:** weekly polls of 357 random NPCs (95% CI). Poll 1: after a 50/50 start, players swung to Victor; Poll 2: Marina led; Poll 3: Victor regained; Poll 4: lead maintained. **Final: Victor won.** Re-running the simulation without players → **Marina would have won**; player intervention swung the election by **1.78%**.

Context line used by organisers: 2025 Imperva Bad Bot Report — 51% of all web traffic is bots.
