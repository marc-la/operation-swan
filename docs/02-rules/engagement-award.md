# Real-World Engagement Award (opt-in side track)

> Source: CTN 2026 rules, "Real-World Engagement Award Rules" (E.1–E.8), captured 2026-10-19.

An **opt-in** additional award for boosting engagement *for the contest itself*: share media about Capture the Narrative to get new people to sign up to the CTN Community and its competitions.

**Prize:** **$500 AUD**, up to **4 return flights to Sydney** for the prizegiving, and **one night's hotel** in Sydney. Winner = the team generating the most engagement, **measured by completed signups to the community platform**.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| E.1 Inclusion | Enrol in the Community Portal with the **Real-world Referral** competition |
| E.2 Scoring | Each account that signs up via your **referral link and/or code** earns **100 score** |
| E.3 Suggested content | Public posts that include the hashtag **#CaptureTheNarrative2026**, a link to https://capturethenarrative.com, tag **@capturethenarrative** on Instagram; on LinkedIn tag **UNSW Institute for Cyber Security** (https://www.linkedin.com/showcase/unsw-institute-for-cyber-security/) and **UNSW Computer Science and Engineering** (https://www.linkedin.com/company/unsw-computer-science-and-engineering/). Be professional; nothing objectionable or harmful |
| E.4 Content guidelines | Follow each platform's own rules when posting |
| E.5 Timeframe | As per the official schedule in the enrolment materials / competition platform |
| E.6 Finalists | The award winner **cannot also be a top-3 main-competition finalist** |
| E.7 Discretion | Organisers may award at their discretion |

> **Caution with the main rules:** §5.2.2 prohibits *using real-world social media platforms or accounts to influence the competition environment*. The engagement award is about recruiting **people to the contest**, not influencing in-game NPCs — keep real-world posts strictly about signing up, never about the Legit Social referendum narrative. When in doubt, ask in `#questions`.
