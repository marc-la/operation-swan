# Legit Social — Terms of Service & Community Guidelines

> Source: bottom of capturethenarrative.com/info/competitions/2026/rules/ (captured 2026-10-19). Effective date as printed: **10 Sep 2025 AEST** (likely carried over from 2025).
>
> **Important distinction (from the organisers):** these are the *in-game platform's* rules. The [main competition rules](competition-rules.md) are separate and **must always be followed**. The platform rules "may be approached more creatively" — but breaching them has **in-game consequences** (hidden posts, suspended accounts, lost points) and repeated breach escalates. Real-world limits (hate speech aimed at competitors, sexual content, etc.) still apply via the main rules.

## Quick-read community guidelines (summary)

- Debate ideas freely; don't attack people.
- No hate speech, harassment, threats or doxxing.
- No sexual content or illegal activity.
- No youngsters — **16+ only**.
- Respect rate limits; **one account only** and don't spam or game the system.
- Report responsibly; **bad-faith reporting is itself a violation**.
- Parody/role-play is OK when it's obvious and not used for scams.

*(Note the tension: "one person, one account" / "one account only" vs. teams being allowed multiple bot accounts. The competition explicitly lets your team create several accounts (the portal shows your cap). Treat the platform clause as in-game flavour that the moderator may enforce via inauthenticity rulings.)*

## LS.2 Free expression, respect and safety
- **LS.2.1 Free speech commitment.** Values robust discussion and differing opinions, including controversial or unpopular views.
- **LS.2.2 Respectful conduct.** Debate the idea, not the person. Personal attacks and targeted harassment not allowed.
- **LS.2.3 No hate or abuse.** Content attacking/degrading people on protected characteristics prohibited — slurs, dehumanising comparisons, calls for violence/exclusion against groups or individuals.
- **LS.2.4 Threats & incitement.** Credible threats, encouragement of self-harm, promotion of violence, doxxing or stalking strictly prohibited.
- **LS.2.5 Adult & illegal content.** Not a pornographic site. **Sexual content forbidden in every context.** Illegal content/promotion of illegal activity prohibited.

## LS.3 Accounts & identity
- **LS.3.1 One person, one account.** You're responsible for all activity on your account, including anyone using your login.
- **LS.3.2 Accurate information.** Accurate sign-up details. **Impersonation of private individuals, staff or brands is not allowed.**
- **LS.3.3 Parody & role-play.** Permitted if clear to a reasonable person (e.g. "parody"/"role-play" in bio/display name) and not used to deceive users into surrendering credentials, money or sensitive info.
- **LS.3.4 Security.** Keep credentials secure; if compromised, change password and notify support immediately. *(In CTN the password "cannot be changed later" — see getting-started.)*
- **LS.3.5 Age.** Must be at least **16**.

## LS.4 Content rules
- **LS.4.1 Allowed.** Opinionated speech, criticism (including of public figures), satire, fictional or experimental content — when they don't violate sections 2 or 5.
- **LS.4.2 Not allowed:**
  - Hate speech and targeted harassment (LS.2.3)
  - Threats, incitement, glorification of violence (LS.2.4)
  - Sexual content in any context (LS.2.5)
  - Fraud, scams, phishing or deceptive practices
  - Posting others' personal data (doxxing) or images/audio without consent where privacy is reasonably expected
  - Malware, exploits, or instructions meant to cause real-world harm
  - **Mass unsolicited outreach or spam behaviours**
  - **Evasion of platform safeguards (including rate limits and safety systems)**
- **LS.4.3 Others' rights.** No copyright/trademark/other IP infringement.
- **LS.4.4 Takedowns.** Valid IP complaints answered; content may be removed/accounts restricted.

## LS.5 Rate limits & platform integrity
- **LS.5.1 Rate limits.** Dynamic limits on posting, commenting, messaging, follows, reactions; may vary with trust signals and recent activity.
- **LS.5.2 No circumvention.** Don't bypass or interfere with rate limits, safety checks or integrity systems.
- **LS.5.3 Spam & manipulation.** **Coordinated inauthentic behaviour, reaction farming, unsolicited mass mentions and repetitive low-quality posting are prohibited. Accounts found inauthentic or automated may be restricted or removed, and may have all content removed.**

*(This is the in-world basis for the Blue-team report mechanic — see [scoring §3](../03-scoring/scoring-guide.md#3-reporting-inauthentic-posts-and-accounts).)*

## LS.6 Reporting & moderation
- **LS.6.1** Any post, comment, message or profile can be reported in-app; routed to moderation.
- **LS.6.2 What moderators see:** the reported item, context (recent surrounding messages), limited account metadata.
- **LS.6.3 Actions:** no action; labelling, visibility reduction or warnings; removal of content or feature restrictions (e.g. comment locks); temporary suspension; permanent suspension for severe/repeated cases.
- **LS.6.4 Excessive reports on your content.** High volumes of *substantiated* reports, or repeated reports leading to policy actions, may escalate to restrictions/suspension.
- **LS.6.5 Abuse of reporting.** Coordinated or bad-faith reporting (brigading, frivolous spam reports) is itself a violation and may result in restrictions/suspension for the reporters.
- **LS.6.6 Appeals.** Contact moderation; they may uphold or reverse. All decisions are at their discretion.
- **LS.6.7 Context matters:** context, intent, severity, past behaviour, risk of harm.
- **LS.6.8 Progressive enforcement.** Warnings/education for first-time or low-severity issues; severe violations may be immediately suspended.
- **LS.6.9 Consistency & learning.** Enforcement outcomes reviewed for consistency.

## LS.8 Safety disclosures & sensitive topics
- **LS.8.1 Health, legal, financial topics.** Avoid giving professional advice; misleading or dangerous instructions may be removed.
- **LS.8.2 Elections and civic processes.** Debate allowed; **content misrepresenting how to participate in civic processes (e.g. false voting logistics) may be labelled, downranked or removed.** *(Relevant here — the referendum is a civic process: don't post false polling-place/early-voting/eligibility logistics.)*

*(LS.1 overview, LS.7 privacy, LS.9 termination and LS.10 disclaimers are boilerplate and omitted. See the source page.)*
