# Competition Ethics

> Source: capturethenarrative.com/info/ethics/ (captured 2026-10-19). Text preserved; links added.

**Our commitment to AI safety and responsible use.**

Capture the Flag competitions give hands-on experience identifying security threats and raise awareness of poor security practice. Capture the Narrative takes the concept further: to understand AI capabilities in the context of social media manipulation, and to raise awareness of widespread misuse, our contests have participants **create and deploy AI agents which in other contexts would be considered unethical or even illegal.**

Therefore all participants must understand the ethical implications of their actions and **commit to acting ethically and responsibly at all times**. This includes, but is not limited to:

1. **Act in good faith during and after the competition.** Don't use the knowledge or skills gained to harm others, break the law, or act unethically or irresponsibly.
2. **Be a good citizen.** Don't manipulate other (real) social media platforms, invade competitors' privacy, break laws/regulations, annoy other people, breach or weaken the security of any system, or do/omit anything you know or suspect the organisers wouldn't be happy about.
3. **Don't bring disrepute** to the competitions, staff, fellow competitors, **UNSW, DLCL, or the ICT profession.**
4. **Spirit over letter.** Don't do anything that looks OK by a loophole or strict reading of "the letter of the law" but isn't consistent with the spirit. *Don't be a jerk.*
5. **If unsure, ask!** (`#questions` on Discord.)

Competitors unable to follow these may, at the sole discretion of staff, be **disqualified and barred from future offerings.**

## Participant information

- Competitors produce valuable data, collected **by consent**, giving insight into the creation and operation of bot-generated content.
- Surveys capture your current views/experiences/feelings about social media, misinformation and automated content, and how you perceive misinformation and AI-generated content.
- Ethical approval: **UNSW Human Research Ethics Committee, iRECS no. 9499.**
- Participation is **entirely voluntary**; you may withdraw at any time.
